# Pickleball Tournament — Phone Guide

**https://vdua-ocrolus.github.io/pickle/**

---

## Internet: the short version

| | Internet needed? |
| --- | --- |
| Setting the phone up, the day before | **Yes — once** |
| Running the whole tournament | **No** |

Open the link once at home or anywhere with signal. After that the app lives on your
phone and runs with no bars, no wifi, no data.

---

## Before you leave (2 minutes, needs internet)

1. Open **https://vdua-ocrolus.github.io/pickle/** in Safari or Chrome.
2. Look at the bottom of the screen for **"Works offline"**. That is your confirmation
   the phone has everything it needs. If you do not see it, refresh once.
3. Add it to your home screen so it opens like a real app:
   - **iPhone:** Share button (box with an arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → Add
   - **Android:** menu (⋮) → **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**
4. Test it: turn on **Airplane Mode** and open the app from your home screen. If it
   opens, you are ready.

---

## At the courts (no internet needed)

**Set up your tournament**

1. Open the app. Two tabs at the top: **Advanced** and **Intermediate**. Tap the one
   you are running.
2. **Setup** tab → type or paste your player names (6 to 24 players).
3. Set **Courts** to how many courts you actually have.
4. Tap **Generate schedule**.

**Run it**

5. **Schedule** tab shows every round: who partners who, and who sits out that round.
6. After each game, tap the two score boxes and type the result. Games are to 9,
   win by 1 — so one team gets 9. It saves the moment you type it.
7. **Standings** tab shows the live rankings any time.

**Finish it**

8. When all games are played, go to **Finals**. The top 4 players are already filled in.
   Tap **Start finals**.
9. Three more games, then the app shows your **two champions**.

## Things worth knowing

**Each phone keeps its own scores.** There is no syncing. Whoever is scoring a
tournament keeps that phone for the whole event. Two tournaments = two phones, or use
both tabs on one phone.

**Do not clear your browser data** during the event — that erases the scores.

**Do not use Private / Incognito mode** — it forgets everything when you close it.

**A wrong score gets rejected.** If you type 8–6, the box turns red, because that game
could not have ended 8–6 when you play to 9. Type the real final score.

**Made a mistake?** Just tap the score and type over it. Standings update instantly.

**Battery.** The app uses very little, but bring a charger — a dead phone is the only
real way to lose the scores.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Blank page at the courts | You skipped the setup step. Find a signal, load it once, look for "Works offline". |
| Wrong player names | **Setup** tab → tap any name to edit, or ✕ to remove. |
| Want different pairings | **Setup** → **Regenerate schedule**. This erases scores already entered. |
| Scores are wrong / start over | **Schedule** → **Clear all scores** keeps the pairings. **Setup** → **Reset tournament** starts fresh. |
| Want a backup mid-event | **Data** tab → **Save**. Gives you a restore point you can come back to. |

---

## Want to practise first?

**Data** tab → **Fill random scores**. It plays a whole tournament out instantly so you
can see how standings and finals work. Then **Kill everything** to clear it before the
real event.
