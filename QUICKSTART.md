# Pickleball Tournament — How To Use It

**https://vdua-ocrolus.github.io/pickle/**

---

## Internet: the short version

| | Internet needed? |
| --- | --- |
| Setting your phone up, day before | **Yes — once** |
| Running the whole tournament | **No** |

Open the link once at home. After that the app lives on your phone and works with no
bars, no wifi, no data.

---

# Part 1 — Before you leave

Two minutes, and you need internet for it.

1. Open **https://vdua-ocrolus.github.io/pickle/** in Safari or Chrome.
2. Look at the bottom of the screen for **"Works offline"**. That is your proof the
   phone has everything. Not there? Refresh once.
3. Add it to your home screen so it opens like a normal app:
   - **iPhone:** Share button (box with an arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → Add
   - **Android:** menu (⋮) → **Install app**
4. **Test it.** Turn on Airplane Mode and open it from your home screen. If it opens,
   you are ready for the day.

---

# Part 2 — At the courts

No internet needed for any of this.

### Set up

1. Open the app. Two tabs at the top: **Advanced** and **Intermediate**. Tap the one
   you are running.
2. **Setup** tab → type or paste your player names. Anywhere from 6 to 24 people.
3. Set **Courts** to how many courts you actually have.
4. Tap **Generate schedule**.

### Run it

5. **Schedule** tab lists every round — who partners who, and who sits out.
6. After each game, tap the two boxes and type the score. Games are to 9, win by 1, so
   one team gets 9. It saves the instant you type it.
7. **Standings** tab shows live rankings whenever you want them.

### Finish it

8. Once every game has a score, go to **Finals**. The top 4 are already filled in.
   Tap **Start finals**.
9. Three more games. The app then shows your **two champions**.

# Part 3 — Letting players watch

**Data** tab → **Show standings QR**.

Hold up the code. People scan it with their phone camera and see the standings. They
cannot change anything.

It is a snapshot, so show a fresh code after each round.

> **Tell players this at the start:** open the tournament link once **while you still
> have signal** — at registration, or the night before. After that their phone has the
> app and scanning works with no internet at all. Anyone who has never opened it and has
> no signal will just get a blank page.

## Things worth knowing

**Each phone keeps its own scores.** Nothing syncs. Whoever scores a tournament keeps
that phone all day. Two tournaments = two phones, or run both tabs on one phone.

**Do not clear your browser data** during the event. That erases the scores.

**Do not use Private / Incognito mode.** It forgets everything when you close it.

**A wrong score gets rejected.** Type 8–6 and the box turns red, because that game could
not have ended 8–6 when you play to 9. Type the real final score.

**Made a mistake?** Tap the score and type over it. Standings update instantly.

**Bring a charger.** A dead phone is the only realistic way to lose the scores.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Blank page at the courts | You skipped Part 1. Find a signal, load it once, look for "Works offline". |
| Wrong player name | **Setup** → tap the name to edit it, or ✕ to remove them. |
| Want different pairings | **Setup** → **Regenerate schedule**. Erases scores already entered. |
| Scores are a mess | **Schedule** → **Clear all scores** keeps the pairings. **Setup** → **Reset tournament** starts fresh. |
| Want a safety net mid-event | **Data** → **Save**. Creates a restore point you can return to. |
| A player's scan shows a blank page | Their phone never loaded the app and has no signal. They need to open the link once with a connection. |

---

## Want to practise first?

**Data** tab → **Fill random scores**. Plays a whole tournament out instantly so you can
see how standings and finals behave. Then **Kill everything** to clear it before the real
event.
